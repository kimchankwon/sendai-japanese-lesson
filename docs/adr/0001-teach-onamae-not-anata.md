# 0001. Teach お名前は何ですか, not あなたの名前は何ですか

## Context
The lesson spec explicitly requested `Anata no namae wa nandesuka`. It is grammatically correct
and appears in beginner textbooks, so its absence from the Deck will read as an omission.

## Decision
Drop あなた entirely. Teach `Onamae wa nan desu ka` (お名前は何ですか). あなた is not footnoted
or shown anywhere in the Deck.

## Reason
あなた directed at a stranger reads cold or confrontational in Japanese, which directly
contradicts the Cultural Sensitivity expectation the same Deck sets. Students will memorise
exactly one way to ask this question and use it on real people in Sendai; the trade-off is
textbook familiarity for the teacher against naturalness for the students, and the students
are the ones who have to say it out loud.
