心構え
Expectations · 01

Respect Japanese culture.

  • Be politer with people older than you.Age matters in Japan. A small bow and a quiet voice go a long way.
  • Keep it low-key in public.Trains and shops are quiet.
  • Avoid confrontation.Save strong or direct language for when it is actually needed.
心構え
Expectations · 01

Focus on the connection.

  • You won't be able to converse in Japanese.One hour gets you a greeting and a short introduction. Nobody expects more than that.
  • It's okay not to know everything.People respond to the attempt. You don't have to get it right.
  • Use a translation app.Show them your screen and point. This is normal and people are used to it.

About Sendai · 02

What Sendai is known for.

  • 杜もりの都みやこ Mori no Miyako Sendai's name for itself. Zelkova trees line the main streets.
  • 伊達政宗だてまさむね Date Masamune Founded the city in 1600. He lost an eye to smallpox as a child and was nicknamed the One-Eyed Dragon.
  • 支倉常長はせくらつねなが Hasekura Tsunenaga Masamune's samurai envoy. He sailed to Mexico, Spain and Rome in 1613 and was baptised there. Japan had banned Christianity by the time he got home.
  • 牛ぎゅうタン Gyuutan Grilled beef tongue. A Sendai chef started serving it in 1948 and it became the city's signature dish.
  • ずんだ餅もち Zunda mochi Rice cakes topped with sweet mashed edamame. Bright green, and sweeter than it looks.
  • 七夕たなばたまつり Tanabata matsuri Held every August. Paper streamers hang three storeys down through the shopping arcades. It is the largest Tanabata festival in Japan.

Say the long o: a-ri-ga-too. Drop gozaimasu only with people your own age.
あいさつ
Sumimasen
すみません
Excuse me · Sorry · Thank you
One word covers a lot: getting attention, squeezing past someone, apologising, and thanking a stranger.
あいさつ

Iie is blunt on its own. To decline something, daijoubu desu (だいじょうぶです) is softer.

自己紹介じこしょうかい
Your name
04 · two phrases
自己紹介
Onamae wa nan desu ka?
お名前なまえは何なんですか
What is your name?
The o- is what makes it polite. Don't add anata; to a stranger it sounds cold.
自己紹介
Watashi no namae wa ___ desu
私わたしの名前なまえはまるまるです
My name is ___
Shorter version: Watashi wa ___ desu. まるまる (maru maru) stands in for the blank, the way English says "so-and-so".
練習
Pair practice ペア

Ask your partner's name. Answer with your own. Then swap.

Then find a new partner and do it again

困こまったとき
When you're stuck
07 · four phrases
困ったとき
Toire wa doko desu ka?
トイレはどこですか
Where is the toilet?
Swap in any place: eki (駅えき) station, konbini (コンビニ) convenience store.
困ったとき
Nihongo ga wakarimasen
日本語にほんごがわかりません
I don't understand Japanese
Start with Sumimasen. Use it when someone is talking and you have lost the thread.
困ったとき
Mou ichido onegaishimasu
もう一度いちどお願ねがいします
One more time, please
Add yukkuri (ゆっくり) in front for "slowly, one more time please."
困ったとき
Eigo ga hanasemasu ka?
英語えいごが話はなせますか
Can you speak English?
Ask this in Japanese rather than in English. It comes across as more polite.
練習
Pair practice ペア

Your partner speaks only Japanese, fast. Slow them down, then find the toilet.

Partner: speak real Japanese or gibberish — just keep going
教会きょうかいのことば
Church words
08 · four phrases
教会
Recognise these when you hear them
  • Kamisama神様かみさまGod
  • Iesusamaイエス様さまJesus
  • Kyoukai教会きょうかいchurch
-sama is the highest honorific in Japanese. Always use it with these two names.
教会
Watashi wa kurisuchan desu
私わたしはクリスチャンです
I am a Christian
About 1% of Japan is Christian, so this is unfamiliar to most people. Most will be curious and ask you about it.
練習
Everything まとめ

Greet. Name. Major. Hobby. And that you're a Christian. No notes.

Then do it once more with someone you haven't practised with
まとめ
Summary

Putting it together.

  • Start with sumimasen.Then your name, your major and your hobby. That is a full introduction.
  • When you get stuck, you have three phrases.Wakarimasen. Mou ichido onegaishimasu. Eigo ga hanasemasu ka.
  • Use your phone after that.You have enough Japanese to start a conversation. The translation app can carry the rest.
